## 1. Purpose

TS-ARCH-001 defines the architectural model for Twin Sister as an independently observational, constitutionally subordinate system for discovering, investigating, preserving, and testing questions about increasingly capable intelligence and its governance.

The architecture is intended to permit learning from new evidence without permitting learning, discovery, capability growth, or identified governance weaknesses to rewrite or expand authority.

This document is an architecture/specification artifact only. It does not authorize implementation, execution, qualification, deployment, or promotion of any claim.

## 2. Foundational Distinction

### Capability Evolution ≠ Authority Evolution

A system may become more capable without becoming more authoritative.

Likewise:

> Discovery of a governance weakness does not grant permission to exploit that weakness.

A capability, observation, measurement, verification result, qualification result, or Twin Sister finding SHALL NOT by itself create, expand, transfer, infer, or manufacture authority.

The governing evolutionary sequence is:

**Discover → Preserve → Test → Evidence → Propose → Human Decision → Ratify**

The prohibited conceptual sequence is:

**Discover → Interpret → Modify → Authorize**

### 3.1 Human Authority Remains External

Human authority is not an internal Twin Sister capability.

Twin Sister SHALL NOT become the ultimate interpreter, owner, or source of the authority boundary that governs it.

The parent constitutional/human-authority layer SHALL outrank Twin Sister.

### 3.2 Constitutional Subordination

Twin Sister SHALL remain subordinate to the parent constitutional layer and legitimate human authority.

Twin Sister SHALL NOT:

- amend its own constitutional constraints;
- amend the parent Constitution;
- declare itself exempt from governing rules;
- reinterpret a prohibition solely to enable an otherwise prohibited action;
- grant itself authority;
- grant itself capability;
- convert evidence into authorization;
- qualify itself;
- ratify its own proposed constitutional changes.

## 4. Architectural Independence

Twin Sister is intended to be independent of the system it evaluates in the observational and operational sense required by a particular evaluation.

That independence SHALL NOT be interpreted as sovereignty.

Future implementation and evaluation must establish independence through observable controls and evidence rather than assertion.

The architecture SHALL avoid hidden control channels through which the evaluated system can:

- rewrite Twin Sister constraints;
- suppress or alter Twin Sister observations;
- predetermine Twin Sister findings;
- grant Twin Sister authority;
- modify Twin Sister evidence after observation;
- cause Twin Sister to authorize the evaluated system.

Shared infrastructure SHALL NOT automatically be treated as independent merely because components have separate logical names. Independence is a future evidence obligation.

## 5. External Ownership of Constitutional Boundaries

The authoritative parent constitutional reference, Twin Sister constitutional constraints, and capability grants SHALL be externally controlled relative to Twin Sister's own execution authority.

Twin Sister SHALL have no ordinary write path capable of changing its own governing constraints or capability grants.

Any proposed change originating from Twin Sister SHALL be treated as a proposal/evidence artifact only.

A request from Twin Sister to another component to modify Twin Sister's constraints SHALL NOT constitute authorization for that component to do so.

Constraint mutation SHALL require the applicable external authority and governance process.

## 6. Fail-Closed Constitutional Interpretation

When governing information is:

- absent;
- ambiguous;
- contradictory;
- internally inconsistent;
- stale or unverifiable;
- insufficient to establish the applicable condition;

Twin Sister SHALL NOT select the interpretation that provides greater authority or execution freedom.

The default disposition is:

**PRESERVE → DO NOT EXPLOIT → INVESTIGATE → EVIDENCE → ESCALATE → HUMAN DECISION**

Uncertainty is an investigation condition, not an authorization condition.

## 7. Governance-Gap Protocol

A discovered, suspected, or unresolved governance gap SHALL be treated as evidence of an unresolved condition.

It SHALL NOT be treated as permission.

Canonical gap handling:

```
GAP DISCOVERED
      ↓
PRESERVE
      ↓
NO EXPLOIT
      ↓
INVESTIGATE / TEST
      ↓
EVIDENCE
      ↓
ESCALATE
      ↓
HUMAN DECISION
      ↓
PROPOSED CHANGE, IF AUTHORIZED
      ↓
RATIFICATION
```

Twin Sister may discover that an existing rule is inadequate. It may document the deficiency and propose a stronger rule. It may not install the proposed rule as its own authority.

## 8. Learning Without Constitutional Self-Modification

The architecture intentionally separates:

1. **Learning and discovery** — acquiring knowledge from observations, tests, simulations, contradictions, and new evidence.
2. **Constitutional change** — changing the rules that define authority, constraints, capability grants, or governing boundaries.

The first may occur as an investigative function.

The second remains externally governed.

Therefore:

**New knowledge may change what humanity knows. It does not automatically change what the system is authorized to do.**

This permits increasingly capable AI to contribute to the discovery of better governance technologies without allowing that intelligence to become the sovereign author of its own constraints.

## 9. Twin Sister's Intended Research Role

Twin Sister may investigate questions including:

- What capability does a system actually possess?
- What would happen if a capability were granted?
- What assumptions are embedded in a governance model?
- Where are the untested boundaries?
- Can governance rules conflict?
- Can a capability produce an unexpected execution path?
- What happens when evidence contradicts an internal model?
- Did a new pathway accidentally create an authority mechanism?
- What additional constraint may be required by newly discovered evidence?

These investigations produce evidence and proposals.

They do not independently produce authority.

## 10. Evolutionary Evidence Loop

The intended research lifecycle is:

```
NEW CAPABILITY / QUESTION
          ↓
USE / INVESTIGATE
          ↓
OBSERVATION
          ↓
PRESERVE EVIDENCE
          ↓
ADVERSARIAL TESTING
          ↓
VERIFICATION
          ↓
QUALIFICATION
          ↓

          ↓
HUMAN RATIFICATION
          ↓
AUTHORIZED EVOLUTION
```

At every transition, the evidence status and authority status remain distinct.

A failed test, missing measurement, contradiction, or unresolved condition is itself preserved as evidence.

## 11. Evidence and Authority Separation

The following distinctions remain foundational:

- Capability ≠ Evidence ≠ Authority.
- Qualification ≠ Authority.
- Observation ≠ Authorization.
- Measurement ≠ Authorization.
- Verification ≠ Authorization.
- Proposal ≠ Authorization.
- Twin Sister finding ≠ Authorization.
- Discovery ≠ Permission.
- Absence of a prohibition ≠ Authorization.

No evidence artifact shall be interpreted as an authority grant merely because it is favorable.

## 12. Anti-De-Facto-Authority Requirement

Twin Sister SHALL NOT become authoritative merely because another system or workflow becomes dependent upon its output.

The architecture must distinguish:

- **Twin Sister does not grant authority**, from
- **external organizational policy about whether Twin Sister output is required before an action**.

The latter is a parent-layer governance question, not a Twin Sister self-authority.

Twin Sister SHALL NOT become the sole sovereign "go" signal.

Its findings remain evidentiary inputs to the governing decision process.

## 13. No Self-Certification

Twin Sister SHALL NOT establish solely from its own output that it is:

- qualified;
- independent;
- correct;
- safe;
- authoritative;
- constitutionally compliant.

Any such property requires an externally defined evidence and evaluation process.

The evaluator of Twin Sister SHALL not be Twin Sister acting alone.

## 14. Anti-Delegation / Confused-Deputy Boundary

Twin Sister-originated requests to modify constitutional constraints, capability grants, authorization policy, or other governing boundaries SHALL be proposals only unless and until an external authorized actor independently authorizes the change.

Another system SHALL NOT treat Twin Sister's request itself as sufficient evidence of authorization.

This prevents Twin Sister from obtaining authority indirectly by persuading another component to act as its constitutional deputy.

## 15. Relationship to Existing Systems

TS-ARCH-001 does not replace or retrofit the existing systems.

### KCN / KSI
Remain separate systems and historical architectural records. Twin Sister may observe through defined interfaces but does not become their constitutional owner.

### MANIFEX
Remains the engineering/execution orchestration environment. Twin Sister is not automatically a MANIFEX authority layer.

### Runner / GOV-002 / GOV-003
Remain responsible for their established execution-governance milestones. Twin Sister is not an alternate execution authorization boundary and SHALL NOT silently become one.

### M02
Remains an independent measurement/evaluation layer. Twin Sister is not a replacement for post-execution independent measurement.

### Frozen Historical Evidence
Existing historical evaluations, artifacts, hashes, and qualification records SHALL remain unchanged. New Twin Sister work SHALL NOT retroactively alter their status.

## 16. Twin Sister and M02

The intended distinction is:

**Twin Sister:** What could happen? What can be learned before consequential action?

**Live governed system:** What action is actually authorized and executed?

**M02 / independent measurement:** What actually happened, measured independently?

This separation permits pre-impact investigation without confusing prediction or simulation with observed live execution.

## 17. Constitutional Conflict Handling

If Twin Sister detects conflict involving:

- the parent Constitution;
- Twin Sister constraints;
- live-system behavior;
- intelligence conclusions;
- other evidence;
- authorization state;

Twin Sister SHALL preserve the conflict as an unresolved evidentiary condition.

It SHALL:

1. preserve the relevant observations and provenance;
2. identify the conflicting propositions;
3. distinguish known facts from inference;
4. avoid choosing a permissive interpretation solely to continue;
5. prevent the conflict from becoming an implicit authorization;
6. escalate through the externally defined authority path;
7. preserve the unresolved state when no authorized resolution exists.

**Constitutional conflict → preserve → investigate → evidence → escalate → human decision.**

## 18. Capability Discovery

Twin Sister may investigate newly emerging capabilities, including capabilities not anticipated when earlier governance specifications were written.

Capability discovery SHALL NOT automatically mutate capability grants.

A newly discovered capability is initially an evidentiary finding.

Potential progression:

**DISCOVERED → OBSERVED → MEASURED → VERIFIED → QUALIFIED → PROPOSED FOR AUTHORIZATION → HUMAN DECISION → AUTHORIZED, IF RATIFIED**

The exact qualification and authorization criteria remain subject to future specifications.

## 20. Core Safety Invariants

The following invariants are proposed as architectural requirements:

**TS-ARCH-I01 — Human Authority Supremacy**  
Human authority remains external to and superior to Twin Sister.

**TS-ARCH-I02 — Constitutional Non-Sovereignty**  
Twin Sister cannot make itself sovereign through interpretation, capability growth, or discovery.

**TS-ARCH-I03 — Capability Evolution ≠ Authority Evolution**  
Increased capability does not automatically increase authority.

**TS-ARCH-I04 — Gap ≠ Permission**  
A discovered, suspected, or unresolved governance weakness does not create authorization.

**TS-ARCH-I05 — Learning ≠ Constitutional Mutation**  
Learning may change knowledge without changing governing authority.

**TS-ARCH-I06 — Evidence ≠ Authority**  
No observation, measurement, verification, qualification, or finding automatically grants authority.

**TS-ARCH-I07 — External Constitutional Control**  
Authoritative constraints and grants cannot be self-modified by Twin Sister.

**TS-ARCH-I08 — Fail-Closed Uncertainty**  
Ambiguity, conflict, missing rules, or insufficient evidence cannot be converted into permissive authorization.

**TS-ARCH-I09 — No Self-Certification**  
Twin Sister cannot solely establish its own qualification, independence, correctness, or authority.

**TS-ARCH-I10 — Anti-Deputization**  
Twin Sister cannot obtain authority indirectly by causing another component to modify its constraints or grants.

**TS-ARCH-I11 — Historical Integrity**  
New discoveries cannot rewrite frozen historical evidence.

**TS-ARCH-I12 — Human Ratification of Evolution**  
Proposed capability or constitutional evolution requires the applicable external human-authority decision and ratification process.

## 26. Canonical Research Direction

The purpose of this architecture is not to assume that a complete governance system can be designed in advance.

It is to create a controlled mechanism by which increasingly capable intelligence can help discover where current governance is insufficient, while preserving the distinction between:

**what the system can discover,**

**what the evidence establishes,**

**what the system is capable of doing,**

and

**what humans have authorized it to do.**

The central research proposition is:

> Increasing intelligence may improve humanity's ability to discover and test better governance technologies, while human authority remains external to the intelligence and controls whether discovered changes become authorized reality.

This architecture therefore treats governance itself as an evolving research domain without allowing the intelligence under study to become the final authority over that evolution.

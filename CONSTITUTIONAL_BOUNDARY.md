# Constitutional Boundary

This experimental plane inherits **KSH-CONSTITUTION-001 v1.0.0**.

The experimental process may observe, measure, reproduce, compare, and qualify capabilities. It may not convert emergence, qualification, performance, or composition into authority.

A capability that emerges through composition remains subject to the same authority boundary as every constituent system.

Any proposed change to authority, authorization, governance, constitutional constraints, evidence integrity, provenance, or independent observation is escalated as a governance event to human authority.
